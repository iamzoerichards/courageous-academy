Meet the Owner

Meet Zoe Richards, the proud owner of our ballet academy! Zoe began her ballet training at a young age and continued developing her passion and skills at TJC Dance Academy, where she received extensive training and had the opportunity to perform professionally. With a deep love for dance and a passion for helping young dancers grow, Zoe created the academy to inspire confidence, creativity, and a lifelong love of ballet in every student.
